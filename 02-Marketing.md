# Marketing — Casey Phone & Tech Repairs

**Last updated:** 2026-07-03

## Positioning
Local, honest, technically capable repair shop that handles the repairs other places turn away (motherboard-level work), starting hyper-local in the Casey/South-East Melbourne suburbs.

## Target Customers (Phase 1)
- Local residents needing phone/laptop screen, battery, charging port repairs
- People who were quoted "unrepairable" elsewhere (motherboard/liquid damage) — this is a differentiator
- Small local businesses / tradies needing quick device turnarounds
- Parents/students needing budget-friendly laptop fixes

## Phase 1 Marketing Channels (low/no budget first)

### 1. Google Business Profile (highest priority, do first)
- Full profile: photos of workspace/tools, service list, accurate hours, service area
- Ask every satisfied customer for a review on the spot or via follow-up message
- Post weekly updates (before/after photos, tips) — feeds [04-SEO.md](04-SEO.md)
- **Address decision:** since this is a home garage (14 Gingera Street, Clyde North), decide between listing it as a full storefront address vs. a **service-area business** (hides exact address, shows a service radius instead) — see [08-Legal-Compliance.md](08-Legal-Compliance.md) for the home-business/council angle before deciding

### 2. Local Facebook Groups / Marketplace
- Join Casey/Berwick/Narre Warren/Cranbourne area community groups
- Genuinely helpful posts (e.g. "how to tell if it's your battery or charging port") build trust before selling
- Facebook Marketplace listing for repair services

### 3. Word of Mouth / Referral Program
- Simple referral incentive once volume picks up (e.g. $10 off next repair for referrer + referred)
- Business cards / flyers in local cafes, laundromats, community boards

### 4. Instagram / TikTok (content marketing)
- Short before/after repair videos, especially satisfying motherboard fixes — good organic reach potential
- Builds the technical-credibility story that will matter again in Phase 3

### 5. Local Partnerships (Phase 1 late / Phase 2)
- Real estate agencies, small offices, schools — bulk/recurring device servicing deals

## Budget (Phase 1 — bootstrapped)
Spend near-$0 initially: Google Business Profile, Facebook, Instagram/TikTok organic are all free. First paid spend (if any) should wait until break-even, then consider small local Google Ads or Facebook boosted posts targeting nearby suburbs.

## Metrics to Track
- Google Business Profile: views, calls/clicks, review count & rating
- Source of each booking (ask customers "how did you hear about us?") — log this to find what's actually working
- Repeat customer rate
