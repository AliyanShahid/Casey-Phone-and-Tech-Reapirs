# Legal & Compliance — Casey Phone & Tech Repairs

**Jurisdiction:** Victoria, Australia (confirm all points below with an actual accountant/lawyer before relying on them — this is a planning checklist, not legal advice)

## GST
- See [06-Finance.md](06-Finance.md) — mandatory once turnover ≥ $75,000/year, optional below that

## Australian Consumer Law (ACL) — directly relevant to a repair business
- Repairs must come with a guarantee of **acceptable quality** and repair using care and skill — customers have rights to a free fix, replacement, or refund if a repair fails and isn't due to misuse
- Must be clear with customers about repair guarantees/warranty terms in writing (ties to [07-Operations.md](07-Operations.md) warranty policy)
- If using refurbished/non-genuine parts, disclose this to the customer — avoid misleading claims (e.g. don't imply "genuine Apple part" if it isn't)
- Right to Repair / spare parts disclosure obligations may apply for certain product categories — check current ACCC guidance as the business grows

## Data Privacy (customer devices contain personal data)
- Devices in for repair often hold personal photos, messages, banking apps — treat this data as sensitive
- Have a clear, simple policy: don't access data beyond what's needed to diagnose/repair; don't copy or view personal files
- If passcodes are provided for testing, document consent and only use for the stated purpose
- Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) applies once the business meets relevant turnover/activity thresholds — worth a written privacy policy even before it's strictly mandatory, since it builds customer trust
- Secure disposal: never resell/discard old parts (batteries, storage) without data destruction consideration if applicable

## E-Waste & Environmental (Victoria-specific)
- Victoria has an **e-waste landfill ban** (batteries and e-waste cannot go in general waste/landfill bins)
- Set up proper e-waste/battery recycling for replaced parts (many councils and some suppliers offer e-waste drop-off/collection)
- Lithium battery handling: store and dispose of damaged/swollen batteries safely (fire risk) — don't just bin them

## Insurance (get in place before taking in customer devices — high priority)
- **Public liability insurance** — covers injury/damage claims from customers/visitors
- **Tools of trade / equipment insurance** — covers your own tools and equipment
- **Goods/stock in transit and in care, custody & control** — covers customer devices while they're in your possession (critical — a dropped/damaged customer device without this cover is a direct financial hit and liability risk)
- Consider **professional indemnity** as the business grows into more complex/data-sensitive work
- Home/garage workshop: check whether operating a business from home affects standard home & contents insurance — may need to notify insurer or get a business-specific policy

## Home-Based Business (14 Gingera Street, Clyde North VIC 3978)
- **City of Casey council:** check whether running a repair business from home requires a home-business permit — this typically depends on things like customer visits/traffic, signage, and whether it's a residential zone. Confirm directly with Casey Council before advertising walk-in/drop-off at the home address.
- If customer visits to the residence are restricted or undesirable, consider a **mail-in / pickup-drop-off (PUDO) model** instead of walk-ins, or use a public meeting point for handovers
- Ties into the Marketing decision on whether to list the address publicly on Google Business Profile or use a service-area listing — see [02-Marketing.md](02-Marketing.md)

## Current Employment / Conflict of Interest
- Founder is currently employed as a technician at another mobile repair shop. Check that employment contract for:
  - Non-compete or conflict-of-interest clauses
  - Moonlighting/secondary-employment disclosure requirements
  - Any clause about using skills/knowledge gained on the job for a competing business
- Keep the two businesses clearly separated (no using employer's tools, parts, customer leads, or work time for Casey Phone & Tech Repairs)

## Work Health & Safety
- Soldering fumes, heat tools, sharp tools, ESD/electrical safety — even solo, follow basic WHS practice (ventilation, PPE, safe storage of chemicals like isopropyl alcohol and flux)
- If/when hiring in Phase 2, formal WHS obligations kick in as an employer

## Signage / Terms
- Have a simple written terms-of-service / repair intake form covering: liability for pre-existing damage, data disclaimer, warranty terms, what happens to unclaimed devices after X days
- Customer signs/acknowledges this at intake
