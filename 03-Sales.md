## Service Menu (Phase 1)

### Phones
- Screen replacement
- Battery replacement
- Charging port repair
- Water/liquid damage treatment
- Camera/speaker/mic repair
- Motherboard-level repair (micro-soldering, IC reflow/reball) — key differentiator vs. competitors who only do screens/batteries

### Laptops / PCs
- Screen replacement
- Battery replacement
- Keyboard/trackpad replacement
- Motherboard repair (power circuit, charging IC, liquid damage)
- SSD/RAM upgrades
- OS reinstall / virus removal / data recovery (basic)

### Tablets
- Screen, battery, charging port (same as phones)

## Pricing Structure (fill in real numbers as sourced)
Use a tiered approach:
- **Diagnostic fee:** free if repair proceeds; small flat fee (e.g. $20–30) if customer declines after diagnosis, waived case-by-case early on to build reviews
- **Standard repairs (screen/battery/port):** fixed price per device model — build a price list from parts cost × margin (see [06-Finance.md](06-Finance.md))
- **Motherboard-level repairs:** quoted after diagnostic, since scope varies — always get customer sign-off before starting
- **Data recovery / OS work:** flat fee per job

## Sales Process (Phase 1)
1. **Inquiry** (phone/social/Google Business Profile/website form) → capture device + issue
2. **Quick triage** — give a rough price range immediately if it's a known issue (screen/battery); flag "needs diagnostic" for anything unclear
3. **Booking** — drop-off, pickup, or mail-in slot confirmed (see [05-Development.md](05-Development.md) for booking tooling)
4. **Diagnostic + firm quote** — always confirm price and turnaround before starting work; get explicit yes
5. **Repair + QC** — see [07-Operations.md](07-Operations.md) for workflow
6. **Handover** — test in front of customer, explain what was done, offer warranty info
7. **Follow-up** — day-after check-in message + review request
