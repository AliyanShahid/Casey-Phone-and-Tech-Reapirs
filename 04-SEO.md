# SEO — Casey Phone & Tech Repairs

**Last updated:** 2026-07-03

## Priority: Local SEO first, everything else later
For a garage-based repair business, Local SEO (Google Business Profile + map pack rankings) matters far more than generic organic SEO in Phase 1. Most customers search "phone repair near me" or "[suburb] phone repair."

## Why the Name "Casey"
Workshop is at 14 Gingera Street, Clyde North VIC 3978 — inside the **City of Casey** local government area. Naming the business "Casey Phone & Tech Repairs" was a deliberate SEO choice: it puts the exact LGA/region name in the business name itself, which reinforces local-intent searches ("Casey phone repair", "phone repair Casey") and matches how Google associates the business with the whole Casey region, not just Clyde North specifically. Keep this consistent everywhere — GBP category, on-site copy, directory listings.

## Target Suburbs (City of Casey / South-East Melbourne)
Clyde North (home base), Clyde, Cranbourne, Berwick, Narre Warren, Pakenham, Officer, Hampton Park, Endeavour Hills — build content and GBP service areas around these.

## Keyword Targets
- Primary: "phone repair [suburb]", "laptop repair [suburb]", "iPhone screen repair [suburb]"
- Secondary/differentiator: "motherboard repair [suburb]", "water damage phone repair near me", "phone repair when told unrepairable"
- Long-tail: "iPhone 13 screen replacement cost Melbourne", "laptop won't turn on repair Casey"

## Google Business Profile Optimization (do this first — see [02-Marketing.md](02-Marketing.md))
- Correct category (Mobile Phone Repair Shop / Computer Repair Service — use both if allowed via services list)
- Full service list matching [03-Sales.md](03-Sales.md)
- Weekly photo/post updates
- Respond to every review (good or bad) within 24–48h
- Q&A section pre-filled with common questions (turnaround time, warranty, walk-ins welcome?)

## On-Site SEO (once website exists — see [05-Development.md](05-Development.md))
- One landing page per suburb served (thin but genuine — real service area, not spam pages)
- One page per major service (phone screen repair, laptop repair, motherboard repair, water damage)
- Fast load time, mobile-first (most searches will be on a phone)
- Schema markup: LocalBusiness + Service schema
- NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistency across site, GBP, and directories

## Local Citations / Directories (free, do early)
- True Local, Yellow Pages, Hotfrog, Yelp AU, Localsearch, Word of Mouth (WOMO) — consistent NAP everywhere
- Facebook Page with matching business info

## Content Plan (organic, ties into Marketing)
- Blog/post ideas: "How to tell if it's your battery or your charging port", "Is it worth repairing a water-damaged phone?", "iPhone vs Samsung screen repair cost differences"
- Repurpose into short-form video for Instagram/TikTok (see [02-Marketing.md](02-Marketing.md))

## Backlinks (Phase 1 realistic sources)
- Local directory listings (above)
- Local business associations / chamber of commerce listing
- Partnerships with complementary local businesses (phone case shops, computer stores) — mutual links

## Metrics to Track
- Google Business Profile: search views, map views, direction requests, calls
- "Near me" / map pack ranking for target keywords (check manually or with a free rank tracker)
- Website organic traffic once live
