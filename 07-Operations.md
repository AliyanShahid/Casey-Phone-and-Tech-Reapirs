## Repair Workflow (standard operating procedure)
**Run this fully manually at first.** Founder does every step by hand (intake, comms, quoting, admin) until it's smooth and repeatable — don't hand steps to an n8n agent until the manual version is proven. Also note: founder is still working as a technician at another shop while this ramps up, so capacity is limited early on — keep booking volume realistic. Once the workflow below is solid, back-office steps (confirmations, status updates, review requests) move to agents — see [09-Agents-Automation.md](09-Agents-Automation.md) — so the founder's time stays on the physical repair and customer-facing steps (2, 4, 5, 6).

1. **Intake** — log device, customer contact, reported issue, visible condition (photos for liability), passcode handling policy
2. **Initial diagnostic** — confirm root cause before quoting; check for prior repair attempts/non-genuine parts
3. **Quote & approval** — confirm price + turnaround with customer before any work begins (see [03-Sales.md](03-Sales.md))
4. **Repair** — follow proper ESD/ heat safety procedure; document board-level work with photos for QC and learning
5. **Quality control (QC)** — full function test (screen, buttons, sensors, charging, water resistance reseal if applicable) before calling it done
6. **Handover** — test in front of customer, explain what was replaced/fixed, provide warranty terms in writing
7. **Warranty tracking** — log warranty start date and terms per job (see below)
