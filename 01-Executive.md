# Executive — Casey Phone & Tech Repairs

**Last updated:** 2026-07-03

## Vision
Build a profitable garage repair business as the training ground for revenue, discipline, and automation skills, then use that foundation to launch a software house focused on AI-driven automation and security products.

## Mission (Phase 1)
Fix phones, laptops, and tech — properly, honestly, and fast — from the garage, generating real revenue and building the operating/automation habits that the software house will later run on. No storefront and no technician-hiring plan; the business stays lean and founder-run through Phase 1.

## Core Values
1. **Honesty over upsell** — never recommend a repair that isn't needed; tell customers when something isn't worth fixing.
2. **Fix it right the first time** — proper diagnostics before quoting, quality parts, real testing before handover.
3. **Fast, clear communication** — customers should never have to chase a status update.
4. **Reputation and revenue over expansion** — grow trust and cash, not headcount or floor space.
5. **Technical depth compounds** — every hard repair (motherboard-level work especially), and every automation workflow built to run the business, is also direct R&D for the future software house.

## Org Structure

### Phase 1 (now)
Solo operator covering all functions, starting fully manual — founder does repairs, customer service, bookings, and admin personally until the process is smooth and repeatable. Departments below are planning lenses, not separate people yet. No plan to hire technicians or open a physical shop — once the manual process is proven, back-office/technical-admin work moves to n8n agents (see [09-Agents-Automation.md](09-Agents-Automation.md)) rather than a human hire, freeing the founder to stay focused on hands-on repairs and face-to-face customer service.
